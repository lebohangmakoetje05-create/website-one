What to expect.

Afriski Mountain Resort

Location:

Afriski Mountain Resort is located in the Maluti Mountains of northern Lesotho, near Mahlasela Pass in th Butha-Buthe Distric, close to the village Oxbow



Sehlabathebe National Park

Location:

Sehlabathebe National Park Lodge is an accommodation facility located within Sehlabathebe National Park in Qacha's Nek, Lesotho. It is situated in the remote southern highlands of the Drakensberg Mountains








Semonkong Lodge

Location:

Semonkong Lodge is located in Semonkong, Lesotho, besides the Maletsunyane River. It offers visitors a peaceful highland experience near the famous Maletsunyane Falls
